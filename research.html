Research

Research directions at the intersection of control, learning and vehicle dynamics.

Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence

Learning, Perception and Control in Physical Systems

I am interested in how autonomous vehicles and robots can learn through interaction with their environment. My research directions connect physical modelling, learned dynamics, state estimation and control to support adaptation and reliable behaviour.

  • Physics-informed and hybrid learning, digital twins and world models for transfer from simulation to real systems.
  • Reinforcement and online learning, virtual sensing and safety-aware control for embodied and distributed systems.

Hybrid Learning & Predictive Control

Physical Models and Learned Dynamics for Vehicle Control

  • I study how recurrent models can approximate nonlinear predictive control and how learned dynamics can complement physical vehicle models.
  • Combining learned control policies with online feasibility checks and safe fallback mechanisms.

Links: (arXiv 2026) · (Related C++ MPC)

Communication-Aware Distributed Autonomy

Learning, Planning & Coordination in Connected Vehicles

  • In AIDA, we investigate how connected autonomous vehicles can coordinate in real time when communication is limited, and how local safety checks can support their decisions.
  • Principal Investigator: Agnes Poks; collaboration between vehicle dynamics and cyber-physical systems research at TU Wien.

Links: (AIDA) · (Research platform) · (Funding)

Vehicle Dynamics & Safety

Friction Estimation & Braking Assistance

  • Estimating tyre–road friction potential to support motorcycle safety and vehicle-state estimation.
  • Investigating electronic braking assistance for e-scooters, connecting control design with experimental evaluation.

Links: (AVEC 2024) · (RWS Motorcycle) · (FANFARE)

Predictive & Distributed Control

Energy-Efficient Refrigerated Electric Transport

Fault Detection & Isolation in Thermal Systems
